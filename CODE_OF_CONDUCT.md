# Contributor Covenant — Code of Conduct (short form)

## Our standard
Be respectful and constructive. Reports about people's work must be about the work, not the person. No harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or hate speech in issues, PRs, chats, or commits.

## Scope
Applies to all project spaces: repository (issues, PRs, reviews), releases, and any official chat. It also applies when representing the project publicly.

## Security research
We welcome responsible disclosure. Do not post working exploits or secrets in public issues — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private reporting. Good-faith research disclosed responsibly will never be treated as a violation.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban accounts for violations. Repeated or severe violations (doxxing, threats, malicious code) result in a permanent ban. Reports go to the maintainers via the security contact in SECURITY.md.
